# Experiments on which voter-to-flat links to show

All rules tested here are identical for every apartment. They use the configured apartment name, voter file and roster in the usual way. The production matcher and its outputs were not changed. Detailed row-level results and experimental workbooks are local only and excluded from Git.

The benchmark contains 2,013 historical preliminary voter-to-flat links from four apartments. Agents reviewing the records rated 727 Strong or Medium and 1,286 Unlikely or No Match. The saved current first sheets show 695 of the 727 supported links and 321 lower-rated links. The figures count links, not distinct people; related household members share evidence. Concorde's No Match labels were reconstructed from omitted rows, an approach checked against complete reviews for the other three apartments. One supported link missing from sheet 1 is already on sheet 2.

## Full-pipeline recovery test

The guarded section-five rule keeps the existing matcher and adds a single recovery route: a candidate that would otherwise fall below the review cutoff can be retained as Medium if its address parses to exactly the proposed flat and its polling part and nonblank section have preliminary candidates for at least five other distinct flats. The section count is frozen before the new pruning. It does not require the other candidates to be correct, and excludes the focal flat. The existing specific own-name anchor, candidate ranking, grades for existing links and grey suppression remain in force.

Across four complete constituency runs, this recovered 25 reviewer-supported links and one lower-rated link, without removing existing links or introducing links outside the benchmark. The supported gains were eight Concorde, zero Purnima, fifteen Silicon Oasis and two Tirumala. The lower-rated gain has an address apparently naming another development. This improves recall on this sample but preserves the 321 existing lower-rated first-sheet links. See [pipeline_recall](pipeline_recall/README.md) for details and baseline limitations.

## Selective candidate experiments

Using a freshly generated preliminary candidate set and rerunning the actual resolver, an alternative retained a candidate if its address named the apartment or at least two other candidate flats were in the same part and section. A complete workbook run confirmed 712 supported and 81 lower-rated links, gaining 28 and losing 11 supported links compared with the current first sheets. Its new grading marks 714 links Strong and 79 Medium; reviewers supported 703 and 9 respectively, so the Medium tier in this experiment contains many lower-rated links. Some of the supported links lost are plausible families registered in low-support sections. Compatible own-name anchors were visible for every household link, although that check alone does not prove which specific anchor was chosen internally.

A stricter alternative required either an apartment name in the candidate's own address or a different named-apartment flat in the same part and section. Its resolved candidate set contained 710 supported and 14 lower-rated links, gaining 27 and losing 12 supported links. The stricter option returned no mapped candidates for Purnima. It has not been run through final grading and grey suppression, so those counts are not completed output workbooks. See [pipeline_section](pipeline_section/README.md).

An additional offline screen suggests a possible uniform cross-part family exception to the section-two selection: a selected own-name voter and another candidate at the same flat name one another in a relation field. With an approximate complete-flat text check, six reviewed-supported links lost by section two were recovered and no lower-rated links were added in this sample. This has not been tested through the full pipeline, and the flat-text approximation needs a proper parser. See [pipeline_precision](pipeline_precision/README.md).

These reviews are judgments from records rather than independently confirmed residence. They used location evidence and the same sample was used to choose these rules, so the results are not an independent accuracy test. The benchmark also excludes matches never generated in the historical preliminary candidate list. Assess the promising approaches against independently checked examples and new apartments before changing the main matcher.
